What if there were another way to manage medical costs?

Shopping for health care can be overwhelming—and monthly costs add up. OneShare Health offers sharing programs starting at $65/month for select eligible configurations, with several options to compare.

OneShare is a Christian health care sharing ministry. Members contribute monthly and voluntarily share eligible medical expenses under published guidelines. It is not insurance and does not guarantee payment of medical bills.

How OneShare Health sharing works

  1. Explore a program and its monthly contribution. Choose from available sharing programs and review the exact cost, eligibility conditions, and program guidelines.
  2. Members participate in a sharing community. OneShare coordinates voluntary sharing of eligible requests; individual sharing amounts, waiting periods, and other limitations may apply.
  3. Use available member services. Depending on the program, features include 24/7 virtual urgent care and discounts on prescriptions, dental and vision services.

Two ways to approach your needs

For major unexpected expenses

Catastrophic

$65 /month starting*

Designed for eligible major medical expenses such as hospitalization and emergency care. Includes virtual urgent care. Routine in-person primary care and preventive care are not eligible for sharing under this program.

For a wider range of needs

Classic

$85 /month starting*

Options for eligible primary care, urgent care and other medical expenses, subject to tier limits, visit fees, waiting periods and program guidelines.

Common questions

Is OneShare Health an insurance company?

No. OneShare is a Christian Health Care Sharing Ministry, not insurance. Members voluntarily share eligible medical expenses under membership guidelines. Neither OneShare nor the membership guarantees that a medical bill will be paid.

Does everyone get the $65 monthly contribution?

No. $65/month is a published starting figure for certain Catastrophic program configurations. Contributions depend on age, state, program, household and tier. The Classic program has a different published starting price. A program specialist can explain the current options.

Are doctor visits and preventive care eligible?

It depends on the program. Catastrophic does not include routine in-person primary care or preventive care as eligible sharing expenses. Certain Classic tiers include eligible primary care, urgent care and preventive services, subject to limits, visit fees and waiting periods.

Can my membership start tomorrow?

OneShare advertises next-day start dates for its programs. Your actual membership start date must be confirmed during enrollment. Eligibility for sharing of specific medical expenses can begin later under applicable waiting periods and other guidelines.

What should I ask before joining?

Ask for the current Member Guidelines, applicable Individual Sharing Amount (ISA), monthly contribution, visit fees, waiting periods, pre-existing condition limitations, sharing maximums and the details of which expenses are or are not eligible.

Does it cost anything to ask questions?

There is no enrollment obligation from requesting information or calling a CHF program specialist. Membership itself involves monthly contributions and may include other applicable costs, depending on the program.
